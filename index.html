AI-assisted car damage assessment and spare-parts search

We are developing a tool to help drivers understand visible car damage, look for replacement parts, and compare product offers in one place.

Under development — informational project page

About the project

Finding suitable replacement parts can take time. Our planned system uses photos and vehicle details, such as make, model, and year, to support a preliminary assessment and a more focused parts search. It is not a professional vehicle inspection or a guarantee of part compatibility.

Planned features

These features describe our project proposal. They are not available on this website yet.

01 / ASSESS

Visible damage analysis

Analyze uploaded photos to estimate visible damage severity and identify affected exterior parts.

02 / SEARCH

Spare-parts search

Use vehicle information and part names to look for potential replacement products through authorized marketplace APIs, where access is available.

03 / COMPARE

Offers and chat assistant

Compare listed prices, product condition, and shipping information where provided. A planned chatbot will let users search without uploading a photo.

Current status: Planning and development

This is a public information page for a Computer Science graduation project at the University of Tabuk. Product searches, image analysis, and shopping features are not live. Any future marketplace integration depends on official API access and available data. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by any marketplace.
